How these were chosen

The Lens needs a post you can break down, not one you can merely praise. Four filters:

  1. A decision under pressure, named specifically — not a maxim.
  2. A visible before and after, ideally with the member's own number.
  3. Something left to say. A post that already lands its own point gives the breakdown nothing to add.
  4. Low engagement is a feature, not a disqualifier — the Lens is how a good post the room walked past finally gets seen.

Omiyang William — Learning to Sit Through the Trade

2 days ago· Journalling· 2 likes · 1 comment

Price went up to almost 4R in my favor, and I watched it come all the way back to my entry… I'm still holding because, according to my plan, the trade hasn't been invalidated yet.

The only candidate where the honest breakdown is not a compliment. He is transparent, he is following a written plan, and he may still be wrong. "Not invalidated" is a real rule — and it is also the exact sentence a trader uses to justify hope.

You can hold both: name the discipline, then ask the question the post doesn't — was there a trade-management rule for 4R, and if not, is that the gap rather than the patience? That makes it a Lens rather than a pat on the back, and it shows the room the format is safe to be in. You can be examined here without being embarrassed.

⚠ Timing. Omiyang is the D-095 CM candidate, still under review, and the standing recommendation is to start him with no title and no announcement. A Lens on his post is consistent with that — he is already the most active member by a wide margin — but it is not nothing. Run this one second or third, not first.
